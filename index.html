❔ Help & About

CLIPVEX PRO MAX is a browser-based video editor prototype. Your selected local video is processed in this browser; this page does not upload it to a CLIPVEX server.

1. How to edit a video

Select a video. Adjust crop, aspect ratio, colour, speed and text. Preview your changes, set trim start/end times, then choose Export Edited Video. Wait for recording to finish and use the download button.

2. Why is export taking so long?

This export method records the video in real time. A 60-second clip can take around 60 seconds or longer. High resolution and high frame rates use more memory and processing power.

3. Why is the downloaded file WebM?

The browser's MediaRecorder API determines supported recording formats. This page checks which WebM format is supported. Direct MP4 export requires a different encoding solution.

4. Are AI upscaling and automatic captions included?

No. AI upscaling, automatic speech recognition, subject tracking, professional denoising and automatic background removal require additional processing models or services. This page does not pretend those features are active.

5. Privacy and troubleshooting

Video editing happens locally in your browser in this version. Your browser may still store normal site data. If a video fails to load or export, try a smaller MP4, use an up-to-date Chrome browser, and keep this page open during export.
